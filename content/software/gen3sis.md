A general, spatially explicit engine for **eco-evolutionary simulations**. It
models species' abiotic tolerances, biotic interactions, dispersal, speciation
and trait evolution across dynamic landscapes, so that biodiversity patterns —
species richness, range sizes, phylogenies — emerge from the simulation rather
than being imposed on it.

Developed by Oskar Hagen, Loïc Pellissier and colleagues at ETH Zürich, with
Florian Hartig among the co-authors, and described in
[Hagen et al. (2021)](/publications/hagen2021gen3sis/) in *PLoS Biology*.
