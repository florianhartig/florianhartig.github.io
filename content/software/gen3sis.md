**gen3sis** is a general engine for spatially explicit eco-evolutionary
simulations. It models species' abiotic tolerances, biotic interactions,
dispersal, speciation and trait evolution across real or theoretical
landscapes that change over time. Biodiversity patterns such as species
richness, range sizes and phylogenies are not imposed but emerge as the
simulation runs.

Its modular design makes it possible to implement alternative mechanisms and
compare them against observed patterns. The paper describing the engine does
this for competing explanations of the latitudinal diversity gradient.

**Authors:** Oskar Hagen (maintainer), Benjamin Flück, Fabian Fopp,
Juliano S. Cabral, Florian Hartig, Mikael Pontarp, Thiago F. Rangel,
Loïc Pellissier. **Contributors:** Charles Novaes de Santana, Theo Gaboriau.
Developed at ETH Zürich and WSL.
