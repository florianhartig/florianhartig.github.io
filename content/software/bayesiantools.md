General-purpose MCMC and SMC samplers, together with plotting and diagnostic
functions for Bayesian statistics. It is aimed in particular at Bayesian
inference for process-based simulation models, where the likelihood is
expensive to evaluate and off-the-shelf samplers are often a poor fit.
